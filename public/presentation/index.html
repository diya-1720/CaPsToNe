The Real Problem

Why Do Normal Fitness Watches Annoy Users?

Current commercial smartwatches measure everyone against generic population numbers and cause unnecessary panic.

1. Generic 100 BPM Rule
Commercial watches flag any heart rate over 100 BPM as dangerous tachycardia.
  • Ignores individual bodies: An athlete's normal resting pulse is 50 BPM. A busy student's normal resting pulse is 80 BPM.
  • A single fixed number cannot fit both people!
2. Walking = False Stress Alarm
When you climb stairs or walk quickly, your heart naturally beats faster.
  • Watches are motion-blind: They don't check if you are moving or sitting still.
  • They falsely beep: 'You are stressed!' when you were just walking!
3. Scary Warnings & Cloud Leaks
Sudden red banners and medical jargon induce acute anxiety instead of calm.
  • Furthermore, your private biometrics get shipped to commercial cloud servers for monetization.
Our Big Idea

The AWEN Solution: Personal Normal + Caring Mascot

Moving from clinical surveillance to an empathetic, baseline-calibrated companion.

1. We Learn YOUR Personal Normal
Over the first 3 to 7 days, AWEN watches your daily routine and calculates YOUR personal normal range (μ ± 2σ). It asks: 'Is your body unusual for YOU?' instead of applying an arbitrary rule.
2. Smart Motion Filter (No False Alarms)
We fuse the optical pulse sensor with a 3-axis motion sensor (MPU-6050). If your heart rate is high because you are walking, AWEN knows it is healthy exercise and stays completely quiet.
3. Living Friendly Mascot (Awen)
Replaces scary hospital charts with Awen — a friendly 60 FPS vector mascot that changes color with your body: Green when balanced, Yellow when active, and Amber when watchful.
4. 100% Private on Your Machine
Zero cloud databases. All sensor readings and reflections stay safely in a local SQLite database (awen.db) on your own laptop. Complete data sovereignty guaranteed.
System Overview

How The Whole System Works in 3 Clear Steps

A direct, robust pipeline connecting your finger to the local brain and your web browser.

STEP 1: SENSOR POD (Wrist)
ESP32 Microcontroller Pod
  • MAX30102: Reads pulse and oxygen.
  • MPU-6050: 3-axis accelerometer checks motion.
  • LM35: Measures skin temperature.
  • OLED Screen: Shows live pulse right on wrist.

STEP 2: LOCAL BRAIN (Python)
FastAPI Local Server on Port 8000
  • Receives sensor data every second.
  • Calculates your rolling personal normal (μ ± 2σ).
  • Runs Isolation Forest Machine Learning to spot genuine stress.
  • Saves records into local SQLite database.
STEP 3: WEB APP (React 19)
Interactive Dashboard
  • Draws 60 FPS live arterial pulse wave.
  • Mascot changes colors dynamically.
  • 7-Day Journey shows your custom normal corridor.
  • Empathetic check-ins and breathing guides.
  • Zero confusing medical jargon.
Hardware Engineering

The Physical Sensors We Connected & Programmed

Real biomedical and motion hardware communicating over high-speed I2C fast mode (400 kHz).

ESP32-WROOM-32
The master brain. Dual-core 240MHz chip with built-in Wi-Fi and Bluetooth. Handles sensor reading and web streaming simultaneously.
MAX30102 Pulse & SpO2
High-precision optical sensor. Uses red (660nm) and infrared (880nm) light to read arterial pulse and blood oxygen percentage at I2C address 0x57.
MPU-6050 Motion Sensor
3-axis accelerometer at I2C address 0x68. Measures total kinetic acceleration vector |a| to know when the user is walking or moving.
LM35 Temperature
Reads skin temperature directly in Celsius (+10 mV/°C) via ESP32 analog pin GPIO 34 with multi-sample noise filtering.
SSD1306 OLED Screen
Mini 0.96-inch screen on the pod showing live pulse, Wi-Fi IP, and motion status at I2C address 0x3C.
Dual Wi-Fi & USB Link
Streams over local Wi-Fi (IP: 10.201.146.254:80) or direct USB cable with zero configuration.
Smart Logic

How We Stopped False Alarms & Constant Beeping

Distinguishing healthy physical exercise from genuine emotional distress.

THE SENSOR FUSION DECISION
1. CASE A: Fast Pulse + MOVING (Walking / Stairs)
  • Result: Healthy Physical Exertion.
  • Action: SILENCE! Do not disturb the user.
  • Mascot: Turns Golden Amber (Active Mode).

2. CASE B: Fast Pulse + SITTING STILL (No Movement)
  • Result: Possible Emotional Stress or Panic.
  • Action: Gentle check-in: 'Are you feeling okay?'
  • Mascot: Turns Caring Amber + offers 4-7-8 breathing.
ANTI-BEEPING PROTECTION
Why Did Buzzers Usually Keep Beeping?

When heart rate rises, it takes several minutes to settle down. A normal loop would beep every single second!


How We Fixed It:
  • Hardware Lockout: Firmware enforces a mandatory 60-second cooldown so the buzzer cannot beep repeatedly.
  • Frontend Audio Debounce: Web chime has a 3-minute cooldown.
  • Non-Alarmist Phrasing: Never says 'You are sick', only offers calm support.
Mathematical Foundation

The Math Behind Personal Normal (Made Simple)

Why personal statistics work far better than arbitrary medical numbers.

1. Personal Average (μ)
The mathematical center of YOUR quiet resting state:
μ = Average of your readings
Continuously updates as your cardiovascular fitness improves or changes.
2. Personal Variance (σ)
Measures how much your pulse naturally swings up and down during calm periods:
σ = Standard Deviation
Some people naturally fluctuate by ±3 BPM; others naturally fluctuate by ±7 BPM.
3. Normal Comfort Zone
Your personalized safe corridor is:
Corridor = [ μ − 2σ , μ + 2σ ]
In statistics, 95.45% of your normal resting readings fall inside this band. Only true anomalies lie outside!
