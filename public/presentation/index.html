Problem Statement & Motivation

The Fatal Flaws in Existing Wearable Health Technology

Current commercial fitness watches generate chronic user anxiety through generic medical thresholds and exertion blindness.

Flaw 1
Generic Thresholds
Commercial watches flag any Heart Rate > 100 BPM as abnormal tachycardia.
  • Completely ignores personal physiological variance.
  • An endurance runner's resting HR is 48 BPM; a stressed student's baseline is 82 BPM.
  • A one-size-fits-all threshold fails both users.
Flaw 2
Exertion Blindness
Heart rate surges naturally during stair climbing, brisk walking, or carrying groceries.
  • Without kinetic sensor correlation, wearables mistake physical exercise for mental panic.
  • Causes frequent false alarms that users quickly learn to ignore (alarm fatigue).
Flaw 3
Alert Anxiety & Cloud Privacy
Sudden red banners and medical jargon induce acute health hypochondria.
  • Biometric feeds are shipped off to commercial cloud servers for monetization.
  • Lack of empathetic, non-alarmist conversational companionship.
Core Philosophy

The Paradigm Shift: From Surveillance to Empathetic Companion

AWEN transforms wearable biometrics from a clinical surveillance tool into a baseline-calibrated emotional companion.

1. Personal Baseline Calibration (μ ± 2σ)
Rather than benchmarking against an arbitrary population median, AWEN observes the user's personal physiological patterns over a 3-7 day observation window to calculate rolling mean and standard deviation.

Question asked: "Is your physiology unusual for YOU?" rather than "Are you above 100 BPM?"
2. Multi-Modal Motion Filtering
By fusing optical PPG photoplethysmography with 3-axis accelerometer readings, AWEN isolates physical exertion:
  • High HR + Kinetic Motion: Physical activity (Mascot enters Active state, suppresses alert).
  • High HR + Zero Motion + Temp Drop: Genuine stress (Gentle alert & breathing guide).
3. Living Vector Mascot (Awen Spirit)
Replaces overwhelming medical graphs with an organic 60 FPS vector mascot. Awen reacts dynamically across 5 emotional aura states, blinks naturally, waves, and offers time-aware reflections without clinical jargon.
4. 100% Data Sovereignty
Zero telemetry cloud monetization. Biometric data flows from the ESP32 directly into a local SQLite database on the user's host machine. Clinical privacy is guaranteed by architectural design.

Hardware Engineering

ESP32 Sensor Pod & Circuit Topology

Hardware schematics, pin mappings, and I2C fast-mode bus design.

I2C Bus Configuration
Shared high-speed 400kHz I2C bus:
  • SDA: GPIO 21 (Data)
  • SCL: GPIO 22 (Clock)
  • MAX30102: Address 0x57
  • MPU-6050: Address 0x68
  • SSD1306 OLED: Address 0x3C
  • Pull-up resistors: 4.7kΩ on SDA/SCL.
Analog & Digital Sensors
  • LM35 Temp Sensor: Vout connected to ESP32 ADC1 GPIO 34 (analog read with 12-bit resolution & multi-sample averaging).
  • Piezo Buzzer: Connected to GPIO 27 with PWM soft tones (60ms pulses).
  • Sample Rate: PPG 100Hz, Accel 50Hz, Telemetry broadcast 1Hz.
Dual Ingestion Channels
  • Channel A (Wi-Fi REST): ESP32 connects to Wi-Fi hotspot (Jalebi), hosts HTTP server on 172.20.168.254:80/telemetry returning JSON.
  • Channel B (W3C Web Serial): Direct USB-UART connection at 115,200 baud for zero-configuration offline use.
  • Automatic channel negotiation and hot-swapping.
Sensor Fusion

Multi-Modal Stress Anomaly & Motion Filter

Distinguishing genuine sympathetic nervous arousal from physical exertion.

Kinetic Vector & Decision Logic
Kinetic acceleration magnitude:
|a| = √(a_x² + a_y² + a_z²) − 1.0g
Motion is flagged when |a| > 0.25g.
  • HR > 95 BPM + Motion = True: Classified as physical exercise. Mascot enters Active (Amber) aura. Alarm suppressed.
  • HR > 95 BPM + Motion = False: Classified as resting tachycardia / acute stress. Mascot enters Watchful state. Gentle alert triggered.
Anti-Alarm-Fatigue Protection
Wearable devices that beep constantly cause anxiety. AWEN implements dual debounce layers:
  • Hardware Firmware Cooldown: 60-second minimum refractory period before buzzer can sound again.
  • Frontend Audio Cooldown: 180-second chime debounce with calm modal popup.
  • Empathetic Grounding: Recommends 4-7-8 breathing exercise, sip of water, or quiet pause. Never produces diagnostic fear.
Mathematical Foundation

Rolling Statistical Corridor (μ ± 2σ)

Formulating personalized physiological normalcy mathematically.

1. Rolling Mean (μ)
Calculated over a rolling observation window of N resting samples:
μ = (1 / N) * ∑ x_i
Continuously updates to reflect changing cardiovascular fitness and resting circadian rhythm.
2. Standard Deviation (σ)
Measures the individual's natural physiological volatility:
σ = √[ (1 / (N-1)) * ∑ (x_i - μ)² ]
Reflects how widely the user's vitals naturally swing during normal resting states.
3. Normalcy Corridor
Personal confidence corridor:
Corridor = [ μ − 2σ, μ ± 2σ ]
By Chebyshev's theorem, 95.45% of normal resting readings fall within this band. Outliers trigger watchful state.
Backend Engineering

FastAPI Microservice & Isolation Forest ML

Asynchronous telemetry processing, SQLite persistence, and unsupervised anomaly scoring.

FastAPI Asynchronous Engine
  • Asynchronous REST API running on Uvicorn.
  • Background telemetry poller querying ESP32 hardware at /api/esp32/telemetry.
  • In-memory circular cache guarantees sub-5ms response times to client requests.
Isolation Forest Algorithm
  • Unsupervised ensemble tree algorithm for multi-dimensional anomaly detection.
  • Takes 4 features: HR, SpO2, Skin Temp, and Kinetic Magnitude.
  • Isolates anomalies without requiring labeled clinical training data.
SQLite WAL Persistence
  • High-speed relational storage with Write-Ahead Logging.
  • Tables: telemetry, user_baselines, checkins.
  • Zero latency, 100% offline-capable, complete user data sovereignty.
